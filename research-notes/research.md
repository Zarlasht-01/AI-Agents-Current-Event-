# Research Notes — AI Agents

## 1. What Are AI Agents?

AI agents are AI systems designed to work toward a goal by performing multiple steps. Unlike a system that only produces a response, an agent can potentially plan actions, use tools, observe results, and adjust its approach.

### Key Point

AI agents are focused on completing tasks rather than only generating responses.

---

## 2. How Are They Different From Chatbots?

Traditional chatbots generally respond to user prompts.

AI agents can be designed to:

* Understand a goal
* Break a goal into steps
* Use tools
* Perform actions
* Check results
* Adjust their approach

### Key Point

The major difference is the ability to perform multi-step workflows and take actions.

---

## 3. Applications

Potential applications include:

* Software development
* Research
* Education
* Business workflows
* Customer service
* Information management

### Key Point

AI agents may help automate repetitive or multi-step tasks.

---

## 4. Benefits

Potential benefits include:

* Saving time
* Automating repetitive work
* Supporting productivity
* Handling complex workflows
* Helping users organize information

---

## 5. Risks

Important concerns include:

* Incorrect actions
* Hallucinated or unreliable information
* Privacy
* Cybersecurity
* Lack of transparency
* Excessive autonomy
* Need for human oversight

---

## 6. Social Impact

AI agents could change how people work by automating some tasks and changing the skills workers need.

However, AI agents should be viewed as tools that require appropriate human oversight rather than systems that should automatically make every important decision.
