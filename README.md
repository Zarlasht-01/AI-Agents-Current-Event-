# AI Agents: The Next Step in Artificial Intelligence

College project exploring AI agents, their development, and their potential impact on artificial intelligence, work, and society.

## Project Overview

Our project explores **AI agents** and how artificial intelligence is moving beyond simply answering questions toward completing multi-step tasks.

We explain what AI agents are, how they differ from traditional chatbots, how they can use tools and perform actions, and their potential benefits and risks.

The project also examines the growing importance of AI agents and considers how increased AI autonomy could affect workplaces, education, research, programming, privacy, security, and human decision-making.

## Key Findings

* AI agents can be designed to work toward specific goals.
* They can break larger goals into multiple steps and take actions to complete tasks.
* AI agents may support work in areas such as programming, research, education, and business.
* AI agents can use tools and information to complete tasks rather than only generating a single response.
* Greater autonomy can create concerns involving accuracy, privacy, security, unintended actions, and human oversight.
* As AI agents become more capable, humans may need to establish clear limits and maintain appropriate oversight.
