# AI Model Evaluation Prompt

We are college students working on a group project for a basic computer science course about Artificial Intelligence. For our project, we researched a recent or ongoing AI-related current event, created a short presentation video, and organized our project materials on GitHub.

We would like you to act as an AI evaluator and grade our completed project using the grading rubric below.

Please review both our **GitHub repository** and **YouTube presentation video** before giving us a grade.

**GitHub Repository:** [Insert GitHub link here]

**YouTube Video:** [Insert public YouTube link here]

## Grading Rubric

Our project is worth 100 points total. Please grade each category out of 25 points.

### 1. Content & Presentation — /25

Please evaluate:

* Is our AI topic recent, preferably from the last 6–12 months?
* Is the information accurate and explained clearly in language appropriate for a basic computer science course?
* Is the video organized and between 2 minutes 30 seconds and 3 minutes?
* Do we explain why the AI topic is important or relevant to society?
* Does the presentation end with a thoughtful discussion question?
* Do all group members participate in the video?

### 2. GitHub Collaboration — /25

Please evaluate:

* Is our GitHub repository organized and easy to navigate?
* Did all group members contribute commits?
* Did we use GitHub features such as Issues, branches, and pull requests where appropriate?
* Does our README include the required information, including team members, topic summary, YouTube link, discussion question, and sources?
* Did we use the GitHub Discussion board for team communication or peer responses?

### 3. AI Evaluation Setup — /25

Please evaluate:

* Did we submit our project to two different AI models for evaluation?
* Did we save the complete prompts we created?
* Did we include the grading rubric in our prompts so the AI models had enough context?
* Did we save the complete AI responses along with the prompts?
* Are our prompts specific and detailed enough to produce a useful evaluation rather than being vague or generic?

### 4. Reflection Quality — /25

Please evaluate:

* Do we compare the grading results from the two AI models?
* Do we clearly explain whether we agree or disagree with the grades they gave us?
* Do we explain how AI helped us during the research and project process?
* Do we discuss any limitations or problems we experienced while using AI?
* Is our reflection honest, specific, and supported by examples from our actual project experience?

## What We Would Like From Your Evaluation

After reviewing our GitHub repository and YouTube video, please provide:

1. A score out of 25 for each of the four categories.
2. A total score out of 100.
3. A suggested letter grade, if possible.
4. Two or three specific strengths of our project.
5. Two or three specific areas that we could improve.
6. Any other comments or feedback that would help us improve the project.

Please explain the reasoning behind each score and point out specific evidence from our GitHub repository or presentation whenever possible.

Please evaluate the project fairly according to the rubric rather than giving us a high grade simply because we are students. If something is missing or does not meet the requirements, clearly explain what is missing and how many points we may lose because of it.

If you cannot access the GitHub repository or YouTube video, please clearly tell us what you were unable to review instead of making assumptions.
