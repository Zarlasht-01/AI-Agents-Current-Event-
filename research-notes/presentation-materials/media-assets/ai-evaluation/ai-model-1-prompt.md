# Grading Rubric

Each category is worth 25 points, for a total of 100 points.

## 1. Content & Presentation — /25

Please evaluate whether:

- The AI topic is recent, preferably from the last 6–12 months.
- The information is accurate.
- The information is explained in simple and clear language appropriate for a basic computer science course.
- The video is organized.
- The video is between 2 minutes 30 seconds and 3 minutes.
- The group explains why the AI topic matters to society.
- The video ends with a thoughtful discussion question.
- All team members participate in the video.

Only award points when you can verify the requirement from the video or repository.

---

## 2. GitHub Collaboration — /25

Please check the actual GitHub repository for evidence of collaboration.

Evaluate whether:

- The repository is organized and easy to navigate.
- All team members have made meaningful commits.
- GitHub Issues were used for task tracking.
- Branches were used where appropriate.
- Pull requests were used where appropriate.
- The README contains the required information.
- The README includes the team members' names.
- The README includes a project summary.
- The README includes the YouTube link.
- The README includes the final discussion question.
- Sources are included.
- GitHub Discussions were used for team communication or peer responses.
- The group replied to another group's discussion question if this can be verified.

Please look at the actual GitHub history, contributors, Issues, branches, Pull Requests, and Discussions.

Do not give points simply because files exist.

---

## 3. AI Evaluation Setup — /25

Please check whether:

- The project was submitted to two different AI models.
- The complete prompt used for AI Model 1 is saved in the repository.
- The complete response from AI Model 1 is saved in the repository.
- The complete prompt used for AI Model 2 is saved in the repository.
- The complete response from AI Model 2 is saved in the repository.
- The grading rubric was included in the prompts.
- The prompts are specific and thoughtful.
- The AI evaluation files contain the actual prompts and complete responses rather than summaries.

Please verify these requirements from the actual repository.

---

## 4. Reflection Quality — /25

Please evaluate the reflection carefully.

Check whether the reflection:

- Summarizes the results from AI Model 1.
- Summarizes the results from AI Model 2.
- Compares the two AI grading results.
- Explains where the two AI models agreed.
- Explains where the two AI models disagreed.
- Clearly explains whether the group agrees or disagrees with the AI grades.
- Explains anything the AI misunderstood or missed.
- Explains what the group would do differently.
- Explains how AI was used during the research process.
- Gives specific examples of how AI helped.
- Gives specific examples of when AI was incorrect, outdated, or unhelpful.
- Explains how the group checked or corrected AI information.
- Gives an honest overall reflection about using AI.

The reflection should contain the required sections A1–A5 and B1–B4 from the assignment.

Do not give full credit simply because a reflection file exists. Evaluate the actual writing and examples inside it.
