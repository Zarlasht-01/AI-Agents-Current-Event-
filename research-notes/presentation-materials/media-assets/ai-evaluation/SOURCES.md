**Title:** Announcing the "AI Agent Standards Initiative" for Interoperable and Secure Innovation
**Organization:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
**Date:** February 17, 2026
**URL:** https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/02/announcing-ai-agent-standards-initiative-interoperable-and-secure

**How we used this source:**

We used this source to understand a recent development involving AI agents and the growing need for secure, interoperable, and trusted AI-agent systems. NIST explains that AI agents are capable of autonomous actions and highlights the importance of security, standards, and interoperability.

## 2. News Source

**Title:** Pentagon ready to deploy Salesforce AI agents for admin tasks
**Publisher:** Military Times
**Date:** August 7, 2026
**URL:** https://www.militarytimes.com/news/your-military/2026/08/07/pentagon-ready-to-deploy-ai-agents-for-admin-tasks/

**How we used this source:**

We used this source as a recent real-world example of AI agents being considered for practical tasks. The article discusses the approval of Salesforce autonomous AI agents for certain administrative missions involving sensitive military information. This helped us understand how AI agents may be used outside of simple chatbot interactions and why security and oversight are important.
