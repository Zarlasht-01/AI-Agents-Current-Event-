# AI Model 1 Evaluation

## Evaluation Prompt

We are college students working on a group project for a basic computer science course about Artificial Intelligence. For our project, we researched AI agents and their impact on artificial intelligence, work, and society. We created a short presentation video and organized our project materials on GitHub.

Please act as a college instructor evaluating our completed project.

Please review the GitHub repository and the public YouTube presentation before grading the project.

**GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/Zarlasht-01/AI-Agents-Current-Event-

**YouTube Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ijy0dbhgW8k

Please use the complete grading rubric below. Each category is worth 25 points, for a total of 100 points.

## Grading Rubric

### 1. Content & Presentation — /25

Evaluate whether:

* The AI topic is recent, preferably within the last 6–12 months.
* The information is accurate and explained in simple, clear language appropriate for a basic computer science course.
* The video is organized and stays within the required 2:30–3:00 minute time limit.
* The group explains why the AI topic matters to society.
* The video ends with a thoughtful discussion question.
* All team members participate in the video.

Do not award points for requirements that cannot be verified from the video or repository.

### 2. GitHub Collaboration — /25

Evaluate whether:

* The repository is organized and easy to navigate.
* All team members make commits.
* GitHub Issues, branches, and pull requests are used where required or appropriate.
* The README contains the required information, including team names, topic summary, YouTube link, discussion question, and sources.
* The GitHub Discussion board is used for team communication or peer responses.

Check the actual GitHub evidence where possible. Do not assume that collaboration requirements were completed simply because files exist.

### 3. AI Evaluation Setup — /25

Evaluate whether:

* The project is submitted to two different AI models.
* The complete prompts are saved and visible in the repository.
* The grading rubric is included in the prompts.
* The complete AI responses are saved alongside the prompts.
* The prompts are specific and thoughtful rather than vague or generic.

### 4. Reflection Quality — /25

Evaluate whether:

* The reflection compares the two AI grading results.
* The group clearly explains whether they agree or disagree with the AI-suggested grades.
* The reflection explains how AI helped or did not help during the research and project process.
* The reflection discusses limitations or problems experienced while using AI.
* The reflection is honest, specific, and supported by examples from the actual project.

## Evaluation Instructions

After reviewing the project, provide:

1. A score out of 25 for each category.
2. A total score out of 100.
3. A suggested letter grade.
4. Specific strengths of the project.
5. Specific weaknesses or areas for improvement.
6. A short explanation supporting each category score.
7. An overall instructor-style comment.

Please evaluate the project strictly according to the rubric.

Do not give points simply because a required file or section exists. Evaluate whether the requirement is actually completed and demonstrates meaningful work.

If something cannot be verified from the GitHub repository or YouTube video, clearly state that it could not be verified rather than assuming it was completed.

Please identify specific evidence from the repository or presentation when supporting your scores.

Be fair and constructive. Do not give the project a high grade simply because we are students.
